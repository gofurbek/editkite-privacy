KIDLENS · PRIVACY

KidLens privacy policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Android version 0.1.0

KidLens is published by Aqsaq Studio (AQSAQ SOFT s.r.o.). It provides a small prepared library, local family profiles, parent-managed breaks, optional Android device rules, and a selected-media review prototype. Questions: aqsoft.soft@gmail.com.

Family information stays on your device

KidLens stores the parent display name, child profile names or nicknames, age bands, reading preferences, saved items, requests, recent activity, video preferences, and app-pause settings locally. Parent-entered blocked app/domain lists and device-rule consent are also local. There is no KidLens online account, profile server, cross-device sync, or remote parent-approval service in this release. Use nicknames if you prefer not to store a child's real name.

Photos and videos you select

A parent can explicitly select a supported photo or short video through Android's picker. KidLens processes the selected media locally with its bundled model; it does not upload the media or model results to the developer or a cloud AI service. It does not scan your entire photo library or media displayed in other apps.

Reviewed media is read into memory for processing. The system picker may also create temporary copies in private app cache; closing the preview does not guarantee those cached copies are immediately erased. Android may clear cache, and you can remove private app data through Android Settings or uninstall the app. KidLens does not store selected media bytes in the profile or saved-story library. Your original photo/video remains at its original location.

The model can miss sensitive content or raise false alarms. Video review uses silent sampled snapshots. Review is a prototype, not an age-approval decision or a guarantee of safety.

Optional Android app and browser rules

Only when a parent consents and separately enables the Android AccessibilityService, KidLens reads the active app identifier and supported browsers' address-bar text to match parent-defined blocked app/domain rules. Parent-entered rule lists are saved, but visited URLs and browsing history are not saved or sent to the developer. Browser-text AI is unavailable in this release; the Google ML Kit GenAI SDK is not included.

Rules depend on supported, readable screens and can react after content appears. They do not provide system-wide live image/video blurring. Accessibility permission can be managed in Android Settings. KidLens is not an accessibility tool intended for disability support.

Device authentication and screen lock

Parent settings use Android's device authentication. KidLens receives the authentication result, not your passcode or biometric template. A child who knows the device credential can also access the parent area. Parent access expires after one minute or when the app is left.

Android screen lock requires a separate parent-approved Device Admin permission. KidLens requests only the force-lock policy, not device wiping or password reset. A locked screen is unlocked with the device credential. A KidLens pause affects this app; it is not a whole-phone usage ban.

Children and permissions

KidLens includes child profiles and content intended for children. Parents manage local profiles and optional controls. This release does not provide independent age verification or remotely verify a parent-child relationship. The Android release does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise-location, or overlay access. Older Android versions may require storage permission for the media picker.

Advertising, diagnostics and external services

This release includes no advertising or analytics service and does not send crash reports to the developer. Google Play and Android may independently process installation, security, crash, or device information under their own settings and policies. Opening this policy or another external help page uses your browser and its provider's privacy practices. This page is hosted through GitHub Pages, which handles website requests under GitHub's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local profiles and settings remain until removed through app controls, Android's app-storage controls, or uninstalling. Profile-data removal can require a retry if a storage operation fails. Activity and requests follow the app's retention rules. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data and cache. Android backup/device transfer of private app data is disabled. Files you keep outside KidLens remain at their original location.

Contact and changes

If you email support, the studio and its email provider receive your sender address and message so we can respond. Do not send device passcodes, child-identifying details, private media, or other sensitive information. You can contact aqsoft.soft@gmail.com about support messages or privacy questions. Future versions may change this policy when the app's behavior changes.
