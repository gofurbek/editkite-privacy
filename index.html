EditKite privacy policy
Effective date: 3 November 2026

EditKite is a local video editor published by Aqsaq Studio (AQSAQ SOFT s.r.o.).

Your media and projects
EditKite copies media that you explicitly select through Android's file picker into its private storage. Projects, editing history, thumbnails and finished exports are processed and stored on your device. Editing does not upload your videos, photos, audio or projects to the developer. EditKite does not request broad gallery access, microphone access, contacts, location, SMS or call logs.

Sharing and publishing
Only you choose whether to share a finished export through another app. The receiving app then handles that file under its own privacy policy. This release does not enable direct YouTube account connection or upload. It has no EditKite account or server. Later releases that enable direct publishing will require explicit authorization and updated disclosures.

Diagnostics and advertising
EditKite contains no advertising or analytics service and does not send crash reports to the developer. Google Play and Android may independently process installation, security, crash and device information under their own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion
Projects and imported copies remain in private app storage. Project controls remove the selected project; imported media copies may remain for other saved revisions. To remove all private media and projects, clear app storage or uninstall EditKite. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes private projects and imported copies. Files you separately shared or saved outside private storage remain at their chosen destination. Android backup and device-transfer of private app data are disabled.

Security
Private storage is protected by Android's app sandbox. Network cleartext traffic is disabled. Your device security and any receiving app also affect protection of your data. No software can guarantee absolute security.

Children
EditKite is a general-purpose editing tool and is not specifically designed for children. It does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact
Future versions may update this policy when behavior changes. Questions: aqsoft.soft@gmail.com. Do not send private videos or other sensitive information when contacting support.
